## Legal shield

- `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/disclaimer`, `/dmca` (`src/app/(marketing)/<slug>/page.tsx`) render the documents in
  `src/lib/content/legal.ts` through `src/components/legal/legal-page.tsx`; `/legal/refunds` and
  `/legal/trademarks` stay where they were; old `/legal/terms` style URLs redirect permanently.
- Terms: "Copyright © 2026 Borepix LLC", personal non-commercial license, anti-scraping and anti-AI-training
  restrictions, binding arbitration (AAA Consumer Rules) with class-action and jury waiver and a 30-day
  opt-out, limitation of liability, New Jersey law.
- Privacy: Vercel hosting, essential cookies only (local storage, session flags, the HttpOnly admin
  cookie), no sale of personal data.
- Disclaimer: independent educational tool for Radiologic Technologists; not clinical, diagnostic or
  patient-care advice; no pass rate promised.
- DMCA: takedown elements, designated agent at legal@borepix.com, counter-notice and repeat-infringer
  policy.
- Footer (`src/components/site/footer.tsx`): Privacy Policies, Terms of Service, Contact, plus Refund
  Policy, Educational Disclaimer, Trademark Notice and DMCA Copyright Notice.
- Clickwrap: the sign-up form (`src/components/auth/signup-form.tsx`) has a mandatory, unchecked checkbox
  that must be ticked to agree to the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Educational Disclaimer;
  submission is blocked with an inline error until it is.
